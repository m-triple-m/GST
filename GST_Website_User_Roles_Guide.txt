================================================================
  GEOPHYSICAL SOCIETY OF TULSA (GST) — WEBSITE USER ROLES GUIDE
  A simple guide to understanding who can do what on the website
================================================================


----------------------------------------------------------------
  OVERVIEW
----------------------------------------------------------------

The GST website has three types of logged-in users, each with
a different level of access depending on their role within the
organization.

  1. Member
  2. Executive Member
  3. Admin


================================================================
  ROLE 1 — MEMBER
  (Regular registered members of the society)
================================================================

What a Member CAN do:
  - Log in and access their personal dashboard
  - View upcoming and past events
  - Register for events and pay online
  - View resources published by the society
  - Update their own profile and settings
  - View the executive board and member profiles

What a Member CANNOT do:
  - Create or edit any events
  - View other members' information
  - Access any admin or management tools
  - Contact inquiry inbox


================================================================
  ROLE 2 — EXECUTIVE MEMBER
  (Board members or appointed officers of the society)
================================================================

Everything a regular Member can do, PLUS:

  - Create new events for the society
  - Edit or update existing events
  - View the full member list
  - View and manage contact inquiries submitted through
    the website's contact form

What an Executive Member CANNOT do:
  - Access the Admin Panel
  - Change site-wide settings
  - Create or manage other admin accounts
  - View the audit log or system statistics

  NOTE: Executive Members manage content through the same
  dashboard that regular members use — they just see extra
  options that regular members do not.


================================================================
  ROLE 3 — ADMIN
  (Website administrators — highest level of access)
================================================================

Everything an Executive Member can do, PLUS access to a
dedicated Admin Panel which includes:

  - Full overview dashboard with site statistics
      (total members, revenue, active events, pending
       applications, unread inquiries, etc.)

  - Member Management
      View, approve, or update all member accounts and
      applications

  - Event Management
      Full control over all events — create, edit, publish,
      or archive any event on the site

  - Contact Inquiries
      View and manage all messages sent through the
      contact form

  - Site Settings
      Configure site-wide settings

  - Admin Accounts
      Create new admin accounts for other people
      (requires entering your own password first as a
       security confirmation step)

  - Audit Log
      See a history of important actions taken on the site
      (who did what and when)

  NOTE: The Admin Panel is a completely separate area of
  the website, only accessible to admins. Regular members
  and executive members cannot see or reach it.

================================================================
  SECURITY NOTE — ADDING A NEW ADMIN
================================================================

When an Admin wants to create a new admin account, the website
requires them to re-enter their own password first.

This is an intentional security step to prevent someone from
accidentally — or maliciously — creating admin accounts without
proper authorization.


================================================================
  SUMMARY IN ONE SENTENCE EACH
================================================================

  Member          — Can browse, register, and manage
                    their own profile. Nothing more.

  Executive       — Can do everything a member does, and
                    also manage events and view member data.

  Admin           — Has full control over the entire website,
                    its content, its members, and its settings.
